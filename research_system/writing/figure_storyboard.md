# Figure Storyboard

## Purpose

Convert the knowledge system into a manuscript-ready argument.

## Draft Title Logic

Electrospun nanofiber-supported sulfonated COF proton exchange membranes with coupled proton transport and dimensional stability.

## Figure 1: Problem And Design Logic

Question:

How can a PEM maintain high proton conductivity without excessive swelling or low-RH failure?

Panel ideas:

- Nafion/PFSA limitation under humidity and swelling stress.
- COF ordered acid channels.
- Electrospun scaffold reinforcement.
- Proposed structure: PFSA + PAN nanofibers + sulfonated COF.

Required data:

- Literature benchmark table.
- Schematic of proton pathway and scaffold restraint.

## Figure 2: Membrane Fabrication And Structure

Question:

Was the intended membrane architecture actually built?

Panel ideas:

- Electrospinning workflow.
- COF growth or incorporation workflow.
- Cross-section SEM.
- EDS mapping or XPS evidence.
- PXRD/FTIR confirming COF and sulfonic groups.

Required data:

- Fiber morphology.
- COF distribution.
- Membrane thickness.
- Chemical validation.

## Figure 3: Water, Swelling, And Mechanical Balance

Question:

Does the scaffold/COF design improve hydration management without destroying stability?

Panel ideas:

- Water uptake.
- Swelling ratio.
- IEC.
- Tensile strength and elongation.
- Contact angle if useful.

Required data:

- PFSA-only, PAN-only, COF-only, PAN/COF composite controls.

## Figure 4: Proton Conductivity Mechanism

Question:

Is proton transport improved across meaningful RH/T conditions?

Panel ideas:

- Conductivity versus temperature.
- Conductivity versus RH.
- Arrhenius activation energy.
- Before/after soaking or cycling.
- Mechanism schematic: Grotthuss plus retained water/acid sites.

Required data:

- EIS fits.
- In-plane or through-plane direction clearly labeled.
- RH/T table.

## Figure 5: Fuel-Cell Relevance

Question:

Does the membrane-level improvement translate to MEA performance?

Panel ideas:

- OCV.
- Polarization curve.
- Power density.
- Durability.
- Post-test membrane morphology if available.

Required data:

- Matched MEA test conditions.
- Nafion/PFSA benchmark.
- Catalyst loading and gas humidity.

## Writing Rule

Every paragraph in the manuscript should point to a figure or a table. If a claim has no figure/table, either add evidence or remove the claim.
