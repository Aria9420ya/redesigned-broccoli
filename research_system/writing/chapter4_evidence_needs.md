## A2-Core High-Temperature Papers

Minimum extraction fields:

- COF/framework chemistry.
- Polymer matrix: PBI, OPBI, Nafion, SPEEK, PVDF, etc.
- Proton carrier: phosphoric acid, ionic liquid, sulfonic acid, imidazole/triazole, water.
- Conductivity with RH/temperature.
- Membrane thickness and measurement direction.
- Acid retention/leaching.
- Mechanical/swelling/dimensional stability.
- MEA conditions and performance if available.

## Do Not Finalize Without These Conditions

- "High-temperature" must specify temperature and humidity/anhydrous condition.
- "Enhanced proton conductivity" must specify reference membrane and conditions.
- "Stable" must specify duration, test condition, and retention.
- "Device-relevant" must specify MEA conditions.
- "Electrospun architecture improves transport" must show pathway or morphology evidence.
