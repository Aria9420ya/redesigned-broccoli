# Route Decision Matrix

## Purpose

Choose the first experimental route by balancing feasibility, mechanism clarity, literature support, and risk.

## Candidate Routes

| Route | Mechanism | Strength | Main risk | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAN/PFSA/sulfonated COF sandwich | Electrospun PAN supports dimensional stability; sulfonated COF adds proton sites and water channels inside PFSA | Best match to COF + electrospinning + PEM stability | Low-RH performance and through-plane continuity may be weak | First experimental route |
| PVDF/COF/ionic-liquid nanofibers | PVDF fiber scaffold hosts COF and ionic liquid for high-temperature proton conduction | High-temperature direction; clear electrospinning role | Ionic liquid leaching and long-term stability | Second route or high-T branch |
| Nafion/P-COF | COF chemically anchors phosphotungstic acid to retain proton carriers at low RH | Strong low-RH and MEA relevance | Less electrospinning-specific; HPW chemistry and leaching must be proven | Low-RH benchmark route |
| Continuous SCOF/PTFE | Sulfonated COF forms continuous proton-conducting phase inside flexible support | Frontier MEA performance and mechanical flexibility | Higher synthesis/fabrication difficulty | Long-term aspirational target |
| SPEEK/TpPa-SO3H nanosheets | Sulfonated COF nanosheets reinforce hydrocarbon PEM with acid channels | Useful non-PFSA comparison and lower-cost matrix | Swelling and MEA performance may limit translation | Mechanism/control branch |

## Recommended First Route

Start with:

**PFSA or Nafion matrix + electrospun PAN scaffold + sulfonated COF grown in situ or introduced as well-dispersed nanosheets.**

Why:

- It directly matches the research theme: COF + electrospinning + PEM.
- It has a clear control structure.
- It tests the central claim: conductivity and dimensional stability can improve together.
- It is easier to compare against Nafion/PFSA benchmarks than a fully new membrane.

## Minimum Control Matrix

| Sample | Role |
|---|---|
| PFSA/Nafion benchmark | Commercial or recast reference |
| PFSA only | Processing control |
| PFSA + PAN nanofiber mat | Scaffold-only effect |
| PFSA + sulfonated COF | COF-only effect |
| PFSA + PAN + sulfonated COF | Synergy test |
| PFSA + PAN + COF loading series | Threshold and aggregation test |

## Decision Metrics

Move forward only if the optimized composite shows:

- Higher conductivity under matched RH/T.
- Lower or comparable swelling versus PFSA-only membrane.
- No severe mechanical loss.
- Stable conductivity after soaking or humidity cycling.
- Morphology evidence that COF is not badly aggregated.

## First Experimental Questions

1. Is the COF located on/inside the nanofiber scaffold or dispersed randomly in PFSA?
2. Does the PAN scaffold improve dimensional stability without blocking proton conduction?
3. Is the conductivity gain from fixed sulfonic acid sites or simply higher water uptake?
4. Does the best ex situ sample have a realistic membrane thickness and MEA path?
