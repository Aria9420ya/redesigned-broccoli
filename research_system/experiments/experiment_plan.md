# Experiment Plan

## Starting Hypothesis

In situ growth or stable incorporation of sulfonated/acid-bearing COFs on electrospun nanofibers can create continuous proton pathways while the nanofiber scaffold restrains swelling, producing better conductivity-stability balance than simple COF blending.

## First Route To Evaluate

PFSA or Nafion matrix + electrospun PAN/PVDF scaffold + sulfonated COF or acid-functional COF.

See `route_decision_matrix.md` for route ranking and first-route justification.

## Minimal Sample Matrix

| Sample | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Commercial Nafion/PFSA | Benchmark |
| Polymer-only cast membrane | Baseline processing control |
| Polymer + electrospun scaffold | Scaffold-only effect |
| Polymer + COF | COF-only effect |
| Polymer + electrospun scaffold + COF | Synergy test |
| Optimized COF loading series | Find percolation/aggregation threshold |

## Required Characterization

- Structure: PXRD, FTIR, XPS, SEM cross-section, EDS mapping.
- Membrane physics: thickness, water uptake, swelling, IEC, contact angle.
- Transport: EIS conductivity at multiple RH/T points, activation energy.
- Stability: tensile, TGA, oxidative/Fenton, soaking/leaching.
- Device relevance: OCV, polarization, power density, durability if MEA is available.

## Decision Criteria

Prioritize routes that improve conductivity without excessive swelling, preserve mechanical strength, and keep performance after soaking or humidity cycling.
