# Quality Control

## Knowledge Base Standard

This repository should be true, useful, and auditable. It should not become a polished collection of uncertain claims.

## Accuracy Policy

- Separate facts, interpretations, and plans.
- Keep unverified leads visible but clearly marked.
- Prefer fewer verified details over many uncertain details.
- Preserve source URLs and checked dates.
- Record conditions with every quantitative metric.

## Completeness Policy

A paper note is incomplete until it covers:

- Material composition.
- Fabrication route.
- Controls.
- Conductivity conditions.
- Swelling/WU/IEC.
- Mechanical or dimensional stability.
- Durability/leaching when relevant.
- MEA conditions if fuel-cell performance is claimed.
- Reproducibility risks.

## Effectiveness Policy

Every stored note should support at least one action:

- Decide a research route.
- Design a control experiment.
- Interpret a dataset.
- Build a figure.
- Write a manuscript paragraph.
- Identify a missing measurement.

## Review Cadence

- Before using a value in an experiment plan: check `evidence_register.md`.
- Before writing a manuscript claim: check the primary paper.
- After adding new papers: update matrix, evidence register, and paper note together.
- After experiments: update the route decision matrix and benchmark metrics table.
