# Glossary

## COF

Covalent organic framework. A crystalline porous polymer network with tunable pore channels and functional groups.

## PEM

Proton exchange membrane. The ion-conducting, electron-insulating membrane in PEM fuel cells.

## PFSA

Perfluorosulfonic acid ionomer family. Nafion is the common benchmark.

## IEC

Ion exchange capacity. A measure of exchangeable ionic/proton-conducting groups, often mmol/g.

## WU

Water uptake. High WU can improve conductivity but may cause swelling and mechanical loss.

## RH

Relative humidity. Always record RH when reporting proton conductivity.

## EIS

Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy. Common method for extracting membrane resistance and proton conductivity.

## Grotthuss Mechanism

Proton hopping through hydrogen-bond networks, often supported by water and acid/base sites.

## Vehicular Mechanism

Proton transport by diffusion of protonated carriers such as hydronium or acid-containing species.

## MEA

Membrane electrode assembly. The practical fuel-cell test configuration that couples membrane, catalyst layers, gas diffusion layers, and operating conditions.

## Low-RH PEM

Membrane designed to retain proton conductivity under reduced humidity. Fixed acid groups and water-retentive channels are usually more reliable than leachable liquid carriers.
