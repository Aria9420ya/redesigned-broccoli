## Working Gap List

- Electrospun nanofibers often improve dimensional stability but dilute proton sites unless COF/acid groups are placed on the fiber surface.
- Blended COF fillers can aggregate; continuous COF phases or in situ growth appear more promising.
- Acid-loaded systems must prove low leaching; chemically bonded or fixed ionic groups are stronger routes.
- High ex situ conductivity does not guarantee MEA performance; membrane/electrode interface and gas crossover need tracking.
- Low-RH and high-temperature claims must always report RH, T, membrane thickness, activation energy, and durability.
