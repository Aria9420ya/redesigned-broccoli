## Evidence Levels

- **Verified-primary**: checked against publisher page, DOI page, or full open article.
- **Verified-index**: checked against a trusted index such as HERO, university portal, or Crossref-like record.
- **Abstract-only**: extracted from publisher abstract/highlights; useful but needs full-paper reading before final claims.
- **To verify**: included as a lead only; do not use for decisions or manuscript claims.
- **Conflict**: sources disagree; resolve before using.
