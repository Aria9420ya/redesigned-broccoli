# Literature Protocol

## Purpose

Turn papers into structured, comparable research memory instead of scattered notes.

## Search Sources

Use Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus, ACS, RSC, Wiley, Elsevier, Springer, MDPI, and CNKI when available.

Search terms:

- covalent organic framework proton exchange membrane fuel cell
- COF electrospun nanofiber proton exchange membrane
- electrospinning PFSA COF membrane proton conductivity
- COF PVDF nanofiber high temperature proton exchange membrane
- COF PAN nanofiber sulfonic acid proton exchange membrane
- Nafion sulfonated COF composite membrane MEA
- 共价有机框架 质子交换膜 燃料电池
- COF 静电纺丝 纳米纤维 质子传导

## Screening Questions

For each paper, answer:

- What membrane problem does it solve?
- What is the COF chemistry and why was it chosen?
- Is electrospinning central to proton transport, or only reinforcement?
- What are the controls?
- What are the exact conductivity conditions?
- Does improved conductivity come with excessive swelling?
- Is the proton carrier fixed or leachable?
- Is there MEA evidence?
- What would be hard to reproduce?

## Ranking

Use three tiers:

- **A-core**: directly informs route, mechanism, or sample design.
- **B-support**: useful benchmark or method.
- **C-background**: general context only.

Prioritize A-core papers in chat. Keep B/C details in the file unless needed.
