# Paper Note: SPEEK/TpPa-SO3H Nanosheets

## Citation

Improved proton conduction of sulfonated poly(ether ether ketone) membrane by incorporating sulfonated covalent organic framework nanosheets. International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, 2021.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2021.05.119

Evidence status: Verified-primary for publisher abstract/highlight values; full extraction still needed for RH, swelling value, membrane thickness, and MEA conditions.

## Why This Matters

This is a hydrocarbon-polymer benchmark for acid-functional COF nanosheets. It helps compare PFSA/Nafion routes against lower-cost SPEEK-based PEMs.

## Core Claim

Sulfonated COF nanosheets increase proton-site density and form continuous proton-transfer pathways in SPEEK, improving conductivity while maintaining mechanical strength.

## Verified Data

- COF: TpPa-SO3H nanosheets.
- Matrix: SPEEK.
- IEC: up to 2.34 mmol/g.
- SPEEK/TpPa-SO3H-5 conductivity: 0.346 S/cm at 80 C.
- Tensile strength: 74.5 MPa.
- H2/O2 fuel-cell OCV: 1.01 V.
- H2/O2 power density: 86.54 mW/cm2.

## What Remains Unknown

- RH for conductivity.
- Membrane thickness.
- Exact swelling ratio.
- MEA gas humidity, temperature, catalyst loading, and membrane area.

## Use In Our Project

Use as a mechanism benchmark for sulfonated COF nanosheets. Do not use it as the main electrospinning route because it does not test nanofiber scaffolds.
