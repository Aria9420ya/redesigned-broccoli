# Paper Note: H3PO4@NKCOF-10 Olefin-Linked COF

## Citation

Green synthesis of olefin-linked covalent organic frameworks for hydrogen fuel cell applications. Nature Communications, 2021.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-22288-9

Evidence status: Verified-primary/open-full-text.

## Why This Matters

This paper is useful as a robust COF solid-electrolyte reference. It demonstrates real PEMFC operation with an acid-loaded olefin-linked COF, but it is not an electrospun membrane route.

## Core Claim

Solvent-free synthesized olefin-linked COF can be loaded with phosphoric acid to create a proton-conducting solid electrolyte for fuel-cell testing.

## Verified Data

- COF: NKCOF-10.
- Proton carrier: H3PO4, about 31 wt% loading.
- Conductivity: 6.97e-2 S/cm at 298 K and 90% RH.
- Conductivity: 9.04e-2 S/cm at 353 K and 90% RH.
- Activation energy: 0.06 eV.
- Fuel-cell maximum power density: 135 mW/cm2.
- Fuel-cell current density: 676 mA/cm2.
- Fuel-cell condition noted in article: 323 K, 100% RH, H2/O2.

## What Remains Unknown

- Practical membrane fabrication scalability compared with cast polymer PEMs.
- Long-term operation under lower RH.
- Direct comparison with electrospun scaffold routes.

## Use In Our Project

Use for acid-loaded COF mechanism and as a reminder that stable COF chemistry matters. Keep it outside the A-core electrospinning route.
