# Paper Note: H3PO4@CTFp

## Citation

Phosphoric acid-loaded covalent triazine framework for enhanced the proton conductivity of proton exchange membrane. Electrochimica Acta, 2020.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.electacta.2019.135235

Evidence status: Verified-primary for publisher abstract values.

## Why This Matters

This is not a classic COF paper, but it is useful as an adjacent covalent-framework acid-loading reference for low-RH proton conduction.

## Core Claim

Phosphoric acid loaded into a covalent triazine framework can form a framework network that improves proton conductivity and reduces low-RH conductivity decay.

## Verified Data

- Framework: CTFp.
- Proton carrier: H3PO4.
- Conductivity: 0.313 S/cm at 100% RH and 80 C.
- Low-RH behavior: alleviated conductivity decay at 20% RH.
- High phosphoric-acid loading and low acid leakage rate reported.
- Cell performance: described as excellent in public abstract/highlights, but no public numeric value verified.

## What Remains Unknown

- Polymer matrix identity and membrane composition details.
- Exact acid leakage rate and test condition.
- MEA performance values.
- Whether it should remain in the main literature matrix or be moved to adjacent references.

## Use In Our Project

Use only as a comparison for acid-loaded porous frameworks. Do not cite it as COF-specific evidence, and keep it outside the A-core route unless full text proves direct relevance.
