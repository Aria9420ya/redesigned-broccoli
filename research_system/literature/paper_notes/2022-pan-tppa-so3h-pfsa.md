# Paper Note: PAN/TpPa-SO3H/PFSA Electrospun PEM

## Citation

In Situ Growth of COF on PAN Nanofibers to Improve Proton Conductivity and Dimensional Stability in Proton Exchange Membranes. Energies, 2022.

Source: https://doi.org/10.3390/en15093405

## Why This Is A-Core

This is the most directly aligned paper for the current research direction: electrospun nanofibers + sulfonated COF + PFSA proton exchange membrane.

## Core Claim

Growing sulfonated COF on electrospun PAN nanofibers and embedding the mat in PFSA can improve proton conductivity while reducing dimensional swelling compared with pristine PFSA.

## Mechanism Chain

Electrospun PAN scaffold -> in situ TpPa-SO3H growth on fiber surface -> extra sulfonic acid sites and hydrophilic channels -> improved proton transport -> PAN/COF scaffold restrains swelling.

## Key Reported Data

- Membrane type: PFSA@PAN/TpPa-SO3H sandwich membrane.
- Conductivity: 260.81 mS/cm at 80 C and 100% RH.
- Durability: 4.7% conductivity loss over 264 h.
- Fuel-cell peak power density: 392.7 mW/cm2.

## What To Extract Next

- Exact PAN spinning solution and electrospinning conditions.
- COF growth solvent, monomer ratio, time, temperature, and washing.
- Membrane thickness and fiber mat thickness.
- Through-plane vs in-plane conductivity.
- Water uptake, swelling, IEC, and tensile data.
- MEA conditions: gas, RH, temperature, catalyst loading, membrane area.

## Reproducibility Risks

- COF may grow unevenly on fiber mats.
- PAN scaffold may reinforce the membrane but not form continuous proton pathways.
- High 100% RH conductivity may not translate to low RH.
- Sandwich membrane thickness may dominate MEA resistance.

## Use For Our Project

Use this as the first experimental route template.

Minimum controls:

- PFSA only.
- PFSA + PAN mat.
- PFSA + TpPa-SO3H.
- PFSA + PAN/TpPa-SO3H.
- COF loading or growth-time series.

## Next Experiment Idea

Compare in situ COF growth on PAN versus blending the same COF into PFSA. If in situ growth wins at equal COF loading, the mechanism likely depends on interfacial continuity rather than filler chemistry alone.
