# Paper Note: Nafion-SCONs

## Citation

An enhanced proton conductivity and reduced methanol permeability composite membrane prepared by sulfonated covalent organic nanosheets/Nafion. International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, 2019.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2019.07.197

Evidence status: Verified-primary for publisher abstract and method snippet values; exact conductivity and methanol permeability values still need full extraction.

## Why This Matters

This is a Nafion composite route using sulfonated covalent organic nanosheets (SCONs). It is especially useful for thinking about proton selectivity and methanol crossover, more than H2/O2 PEMFC operation.

## Core Claim

Well-dispersed sulfonated covalent organic nanosheets interact with Nafion to create more even ion channels, improving proton transport while suppressing methanol permeability.

## Verified Data

- COF precursor chemistry: Tp and DBBA are used to synthesize sulfonated COF/SCONs.
- Matrix: Nafion, with Nafion 115 listed in public materials snippet.
- Best reported membrane: Nafion-SCONs-0.6.
- DMFC power density: 118.2 mW/cm2.
- Nafion reference power density: 81.9 mW/cm2.
- DMFC condition: 2 M methanol at 60 C.

## What Remains Unknown

- Exact proton conductivity value and conditions.
- Exact methanol permeability value.
- Membrane thickness.
- Catalyst loading and detailed DMFC hardware.

## Use In Our Project

Use as a methanol-crossover and sulfonated nanosheet/Nafion reference. It is not central to H2/O2 PEMFC route selection unless crossover/selectivity becomes a main goal.
