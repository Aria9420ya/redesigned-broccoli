# Paper Note: Nafion/H3PO4@SNW-1

## Citation

Enhanced proton conductivity of Nafion composite membrane by incorporating phosphoric acid-loaded covalent organic framework. Journal of Power Sources, 2016.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2016.09.135

Evidence status: Verified-primary + Verified-index for publisher/index abstract values; full extraction still needed for leaching percentage, membrane thickness, and MEA conditions.

## Why This Matters

This is an early COF/Nafion PEM route and a baseline for later chemically bonded acid-carrier strategies such as P-COF.

## Core Claim

Phosphoric-acid-loaded SNW-1 COF can improve Nafion membrane proton conductivity at reduced humidity, with low guest leaching reported.

## Verified Data

- Matrix: Nafion.
- COF/additive: H3PO4@SNW-1.
- SNW-1 chemistry: melamine and terephthalaldehyde Schiff-base network.
- Filler loading: 15 wt%.
- Conductivity: 0.0604 S/cm at 51% RH and 80 C.
- Fuel-cell maximum power density: 60.3% higher than recast Nafion.
- Low guest leaching rate reported.

## What Remains Unknown

- Exact leaching percentage and soaking condition.
- Membrane thickness.
- Full MEA operating conditions.
- Whether acid is physically trapped or sufficiently immobilized.

## Use In Our Project

Use as historical baseline. If using acid-loaded COFs, require stronger leaching evidence than this abstract alone provides.
